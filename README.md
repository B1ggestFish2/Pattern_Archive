# Pattern Archive

## Purpose

A long-term research project documenting patterns across human history, cultures, materials, and the natural world.

The archive will collect and organize documented examples of patterns found in objects, architecture, textiles, ceramics, sculpture, biological structures, geological formations, and other contexts.

## Research Principles

- Preserve the historical, cultural, geographic, and material context of each example.
- Record sources so that claims can be checked and revisited.
- Distinguish documented facts from interpretation and speculation.
- Preserve permanent record IDs even when titles, classifications, or research change.
- Track research progress and identify missing information.
- Keep original source material and research notes separate from approved archive records.

## Planned Components

- **Master index:** A spreadsheet tracking permanent IDs, titles, research status, and file locations.
- **Research records:** Structured records containing descriptions, provenance, sources, and analysis.
- **Images:** Reference images organized by permanent record ID, with appropriate source and usage information.
- **Research prompts:** Instructions for AI-assisted research, extraction, and quality control.
- **Website:** A searchable visual interface for browsing, comparing, and studying archived patterns.

## Workflow

1. Identify a candidate pattern.
2. Assign or confirm its permanent ID in the master index.
3. Research the example using traceable sources.
4. Record evidence, citations, and uncertainties.
5. Review and approve the record.
6. Store the record and permitted image references.
7. Publish approved information to the website when the website is ready.

## Data Integrity

Permanent IDs must not be reassigned or silently changed. Unverified claims must not be presented as established facts. Research records should retain enough source information for future verification.

This archive is a work in progress. Its structure and methods will evolve as the collection develops.
